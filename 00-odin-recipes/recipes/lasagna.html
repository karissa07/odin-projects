The Perfect Lasanga Recipe For Lazy Nights

Description

Lasagna is the kind of meal that feels like you put in way more effort than you actually did—perfect for a lazy night.

Picture this: layers of tender pasta sheets stacked with a rich, savory meat sauce (or a simple marinara if you're keeping it easy), creamy ricotta, and lots of melted mozzarella that gets golden and slightly crispy on top. Every bite is warm, cheesy, and filling—the kind of comfort food that doesn't ask much from you but still delivers.

On a lazy night, it's even better because you can:

  • use store-bought sauce
  • skip fancy layering perfection (it still tastes the same)
  • throw it in the oven and let it do its thing

The smell alone—tomato, garlic, cheese bubbling—makes your space feel cozy without you doing much work. Pair it with a blanket, your favorite show, and you're set.

Ingredients Needed

Cheese, tomato sauce, lasagna noodles, and ground beef.

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Cheese
  • Tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • Ricotta Cheese

Step By Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F and lightly grease a baking dish.

  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook lasagna noodles for 8-10 minutes until tender, then drain and set aside. (Skip this if using oven-ready noodles.)

  3. In a pan over medium heat, cook ground beef for 6-8 minutes until browned. Drain the grease, then add pasta sauce and let it simmer on low heat for 5-10 minutes.

  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese with a pinch of salt (and an egg if you want it richer.)

  5. Start layering in the baking dish: spread a thin layer of sauce on the bottom, add noodles, then ricotta, then meat sauce, then mozzarella. Repeat the layers 2-3 times.

  6. Finish with a top layer of sauce and a generous amount of mozzarella cheese.

  7. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake another 10-15 minutes until the top is melted and slightly golden.

  8. Let it rest for 10-15 minutes before cutting so it holds its shape, then slice and serve.
