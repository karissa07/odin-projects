Best Recipe For Taco Tuesdays

Description

Taco Tuesday is bold, colorful, and a little chaotic—in the best way. It's the sizzle of seasoned meat hitting a hot pan, the smell of cumin, chili, and garlic filling the room, and the sound of tortillas warming up while everything comes together.

You've got options everywhere:

  • juicy carne asada or spicy chicken
  • slow-cooked carnitas or flavorful beans
  • fresh toppings like cilantro, onion, lime, salsa, guac

Every taco becomes your own creation—stacked, layered, and sometimes overfilled. The tortillas are warm and soft, the fillings are packed with flavor, and there's always that perfect balance of savory, fresh, and a little heat. It's not just about eating—it's about the energy. People reaching across the table, passing toppings, building their perfect taco, maybe arguing (lightly) about soft vs. crunchy shells.

Ingredients Needed

  • Soft or hard shell tortilla
  • Ground beef
  • Taco seasoning
  • Tomatoes
  • Lettuce
  • Sour cream

Step By Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat a pan over medium heat for about 1-2 minutes, then add ground beef and cook for 6-8 minutes, breaking it apart, until fully browned with no pink left.

  2. Drain the grease, then add taco seasoning and 2/3 cup water. Stir and let it simmer on medium-low heat for 4-5 minutes until thickened.

  3. Warm tortillas. For soft tortillas, heat them in a pan over medium heat for 20-30 seconds per side. For hard shells, place them in the oven at 350°F for 3-5 minutes.

  4. Add the hot meat into each tortilla right after cooking so it stays warm.

  5. Add cheese and toppings, then serve immediately while everything is hot (best within 5 minutes of assembling.)
