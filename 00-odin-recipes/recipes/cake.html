The Ultimate Tasty Vanilla Cake Recipe

Description

A vanilla cake is simple, soft, and comforting—the kind of dessert that feels classic in the best way. It's light and fluffy with a warm buttery flavor and a sweet vanilla aroma that fills the kitchen while it bakes. The crumb is tender and moist, not too heavy, and it pairs perfectly with frosting or even just a dusting of powdered sugar. It's the kind of cake that works for everything—birthdays, celebrations, or just because you wanted something sweet.

Ingredients Needed

  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Banilla extract
  • Baking powder
  • Vanilla Icing

Step By Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a cake pan. If you have parchment paper, line the bottom so it doesn't stick.

  2. In a bowl, mix together 1 ½ cups flour, 1 cup sugar, 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder, and ¼ teaspoon salt.

  3. Add ½ cup softened butter, 2 eggs, ¾ cup milk, and 2 teaspoons vanilla extract. Mix everything together for about 1-2 minutes until the batter is smooth and slightly thick.

  4. Place it in the oven and bake at 350°F for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

  5. Take the cake out and let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then remove it and let it cool completely for another 20-30 minutes before frosting or cutting.

  6. Add frosting if you want, or keep it simple and slice it as is.
