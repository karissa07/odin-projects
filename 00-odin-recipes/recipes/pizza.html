Best Movie Night Pizza Recipe

Description

This is a fully loaded, no-holding-back kind of pizza. You've got a crispy, golden crust on the outside with a soft, chewy inside, layered with rich tomato sauce, melted cheese, and a mix of bold toppings. The pepperoni gets slightly crispy on the edges, the sausage is savory and juicy, the bacon adds a smoky crunch, and the olives and peppers bring a salty, fresh balance. When it comes out of the oven, the cheese is bubbling and stretchy, and everything just melts together into that perfect, hot slice.

Ingredients Needed

  • Pizza dough
  • Pepperoni
  • Cheese
  • Tomato sauce
  • Sausage
  • Bacon
  • Peppers
  • Olives

Step By Step Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 475°F and let it heat for at least 10-15 minutes so it's fully hot.

  2. Roll or stretch your pizza dough on a floured surface until it's about 10-12 inches wide, then place it on a baking sheet or pizza pan.

  3. Spread a thin, even layer of tomato sauce over the dough, leaving about ½ inch around the edges for the crust.

  4. IAdd a layer of cheese, then evenly place your toppings—pepperoni, sausage (pre-cooked), bacon (pre-cooked), olives, and peppers.

  5. Bake the pizza at 475°F for 12-15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbling.

  6. Remove from the oven and let it sit for 3-5 minutes so the cheese sets slightly.

  7. Slice and serve while hot.
